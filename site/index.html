If one power line fails, does anything overload?

An AI ranks all 80 possible line failures on the IEEE 57-bus grid in about 0.15 s. The exact solver then checks only the risky ones. The AI sorts, the solver decides.

Try it: pick a day, press Run. First you see which lines the AI sends to the solver, then the solver's real answer for each, and whether any dangerous line slipped through.
